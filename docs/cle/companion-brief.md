# Job B — the composer companion, in the style of the old app's pony

The little companion lies on top of the chat composer. The old app's one (style-*.png, a pink pony) is
loved for its STYLE: clean vector cartoon, smooth outlines, flat cel shading with one soft highlight,
big expressive eyes, compact "loaf" pose lying on its belly, small props, cute and polished like an
official animated-series sticker. Make the SAME KIND of sticker, but of 碧琪:

## 碧琪 (the character)
- a slender, short-haired, jet-black male cat (see ref-cat.png for who he is — but draw him in the
  cartoon sticker style of style-*.png, NOT as an oil painting)
- big ice-blue eyes with a white catch-light; pointed upright ears with a dusky inner ear;
  white whiskers; a long thin tail; deep blue-black fur with a soft blue rim highlight
- handsome and a little aloof, but cute — never angry, never scary, never human-like
- his prop is a small white five-petal flower (the flower he guards), like the pony's balloon

## Five poses (same framing for all, like style-*.png: 400×235 landscape, lying on his belly,
## head on the left third, tail curling at the right, bottom edge = where he lies on the composer)
- rest.png    — lying like a loaf, paws tucked, calm open eyes, the little white flower beside him
- typing.png  — head raised, ears forward, eyes wide and curious, looking up-right (watching you type)
- working.png — focused, one paw stretched out pressing/patting, a tiny gold quill or sparkle near the paw
- happy.png   — eyes closed in a big happy smile, paws up, small gold sparkles / confetti
- sleepy.png  — curled up, eyes closed, a small "z", tail wrapped around the flower

## Output rules
- each file 800×470 (2× of 400×235), the cat at the same size and position in every file
- plain flat light-grey background (#E6E6E6) with nothing else on it (it is cut out afterwards)
- no text, no frame, no shadow on the ground
