# Taste

Where the first teaser's reviews converged. Each rule came from a correction, so treat these as defaults to start
from and confirm only if the request clearly differs.

## Motion

- One long, slow camera move per scene: a gentle push or drift, never a quick re-frame. A camera that travels between
  scenes (a whip pan) read as jittery; each scene now has its own camera and scenes hand over with a dissolve.
- To fit a length cap, cut beats, clicks, or whole screens. Speeding the motion up was the first attempt and the
  worst-received one.
- The pointer gets at most one slow glide per scene, then its click. Every extra pointer move was cut.
- The opening may move more: the page tipping in from a 3D tilt and settling flat was liked from the first version.

## Framing

- One focus per scene (a form, a table, a list), centred. Slightly zoomed out with some margin read better than a
  tight crop; cropping the UI around the focus (the sidebar, other columns) is fine.
- The app full-bleed. A fake browser window around it was rejected.
- The caption sits below the focus: frame a tall card a little high so the card and the caption centre together.

## Words

- Captions of two or three words, as subtitles: "Enter your URL", "Find competitors", "Monitor your AI visibility".
  Imperative ("Find") over third person ("Finds").
- No counts or numbers ("3 topics", "15 prompts"): they distract from what the feature does.
- The end card is the brand, not a slogan: icon, name, URL.

## Look

- The product's own palette, from its theme tokens; dark mode for a teaser. Accents (click rings, highlights) in its
  primary colour; no colours from elsewhere (a website accent that the app does not use was rejected).
- The real brand icon from the live website's icons (`android-chrome-512x512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`), not a
  logo from a design folder or one drawn for the video.

## What was most liked

- Rows cut from the real capture landing in their own places, one after another (the competitor table).
- A list page tipped back in 3D, close up on the text, panning slowly while its rows land just ahead of the camera.
- Cuts on the music: the click on the drop, the logo on the strongest hit.
